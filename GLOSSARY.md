# Glossary

Terms used by the product, the code and the documentation. French is the
reference language; Arabic and English are the shipped translations.

| Term | العربية | English | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| PAP | الاقتطاع التلقائي الدائم | Permanent Automatic Debit | Recurring, voluntary monthly contribution debited from a Tunisian current account. Not a one-off donation. |
| Donateur | المتبرع | Donor | Person who authorises the PAP and chooses its amount and first debit month. |
| Mandat | التفويض | Mandate | Authorisation given to SOS to debit the account. The simulation never creates one. |
| Contribution mensuelle | مساهمة شهرية | Monthly contribution | The recurring amount debited each month under the PAP. |
| Compte courant tunisien | حساب جارٍ تونسي | Tunisian current account | Required account type for the PAP. Cards are not used for PAP. |
| Prélèvement | سحب | Debit | The monthly bank operation. Scheduled on the 25th. |
| Échec de prélèvement | فشل السحب | Failed debit | A debit that could not be collected; retried on the 5th of the following month. Failures are not cumulative. |
| Parrainage | الكفالة | Sponsorship | Regular support programme. A PAP donor cannot choose a specific child. |
| Enfant | الطفل | Child | Beneficiary of SOS care. Never individually selectable or identifiable through this app. |
| Village SOS | قرية SOS | SOS village | Local care setting represented by the growing pixel-art scene. |
| Simulation | محاكاة | Simulation | Educational model of the PAP. Collects no data and moves no money. |
| Source officielle | مصدر رسمي | Official source | Verified fact from the SOS guide or an official SOS Tunisie page. |
| Fait historique | معلومة تاريخية | Historical fact | Past information kept for context, such as the October 2024 10 DT minimum; not a confirmed current rule. |
| Contenu éducatif | محتوى تعليمي | Educational content | Explanation produced for learning, not an official commitment. |
| Réponse fondée | إجابة موثّقة | Grounded answer | Answer built only from retrieved knowledge chunks, with page citations. |
| Mode curé | وضع القاعدة الموثّقة | Curated mode | Deterministic answers from the local knowledge base; used when the model is unavailable. |
| Mode IA | وضع الذكاء الاصطناعي | AI mode | Model answer that passed citation and format validation. |
| Citation | مصدر | Citation | Guide page and status attached to an answer. |
| RTL | من اليمين إلى اليسار | Right-to-left | Arabic layout direction, applied to the whole document. |
| Dock | شريط التنقل | Floating dock | Instagram-inspired five-tab navigation. |
