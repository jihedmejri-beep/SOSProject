# PAP – SOS Villages d'Enfants Tunisie: Reference

## 0. How an AI assistant should use this document

- **Purpose:** explain the PAP monthly donation system accurately to donors, volunteers and fundraising staff, without exaggeration, pressure or invented claims.
- **Precedence:** payment dates, contact details, form fields and contribution limits can change. The **live SOS website** and the **association's authorized team** override anything in this file.
- **Source status:** this file comes from an *independent* guide built on public SOS pages. It is **not** an official SOS procedure, **not** endorsed by SOS, and **not** a substitute for staff instructions, the current authorization form, donor agreement, finance rules, privacy procedures or legal advice.
- **When unsure:** if a fact is marked "confirm with SOS" below (minimum amount, village allocation, exact first-debit date, contact email), say it is unconfirmed and route the user to SOS. Do not guess.
- **Tone:** truthful, calm, non-pressuring, child-dignity-preserving. Accept refusal politely.

## 2. What PAP means and why SOS uses it

### Meaning
PAP is a standing authorization allowing SOS to collect a chosen donation automatically from the donor's bank account on a recurring monthly basis. The donor selects an amount and a start month, then gives the authorization required for the debit.

### Why a recurring contribution matters
A one-time gift can meet an immediate need. A recurring gift gives a more predictable income stream, helping teams plan care, education, health and family-support activities over time.

- **Planning:** recurring income makes it easier to forecast resources for ongoing programmes.
- **Continuity:** children's needs exist throughout the year, not only during campaigns or holidays.
- **Efficiency:** once the authorization is set up, the donor does not need to initiate a new bank transfer each month.
- **Flexibility:** per the public page, the donor can adapt, pause or stop the contribution by contacting the team.

### "Long-term" ≠ "locked in"
SOS describes sponsorship as a commitment made through PAP to support children over the long term. "Long-term" describes the value of continuity; it does **not** mean an irrevocable or lifetime agreement. The same public page explicitly describes the option to adjust, suspend or stop.

**Good way to say it:**
> "You choose an amount that suits your budget. It is collected monthly from your Tunisian current account, and you can contact SOS to change, pause or stop it."

## 3. What regular support helps make possible

SOS Villages d'Enfants Tunisie works to protect children who have lost parental care or are at risk of losing it. It describes family-based care, family strengthening, education, health and community assistance. The aim is a safe, stable family environment for children and helping vulnerable families stay together where possible.

| Area | What it can mean in practice |
|---|---|
| A stable home | A safe, caring, predictable living environment for children who need alternative care. |
| Education | School attendance, educational support, supplies, transport or other practical help that supports learning. |
| Health and wellbeing | Medical follow-up, health services, nutrition, social or psychological support, depending on assessed needs. |
| Family strengthening | Support to vulnerable families so children can remain with their families in a safer, more stable environment. |
| Community assistance | Local projects addressing barriers for children and families (housing conditions, access to services, transport). |

SOS's public project pages illustrate needs such as home rehabilitation for families in its family-strengthening programme and transport helping children reach school. These are **examples of the association's work**, not a promise that a particular donor's monthly amount buys a specific item or pays for one named child.

> **Avoid false precision.** Do not say "your TND 20 pays for one child's food for a month," and do not assign a fixed cost to a school bag, meal or medical visit unless SOS has supplied an approved, current impact calculation. Explain the **broad areas** supported instead.

## 4. What PAP is – and is not

| PAP **is** | PAP **is not** |
|---|---|
| A voluntary recurring monthly contribution. | A mandatory payment or a lifetime legal obligation. |
| A bank-account debit authorized by the donor. | A card subscription or a cash collection at signup. |
| A way to help SOS plan and sustain support over time. | A guarantee that a specific amount is spent on one specific child. |
| A sponsorship/support relationship with SOS as an organization. | A private arrangement giving the donor access to a child. |
| A contribution that can be changed, paused or stopped by contacting SOS. | A promise that a missed debit will be charged cumulatively later. |
| A process that protects children's privacy. | Permission to visit villages, meet children or receive their private identifying details. |

### Specific-child sponsorship and privacy
SOS's public sponsorship page states that donors **cannot choose or receive the identity of a specific child**. Personal information about sponsored children is not disclosed for privacy reasons, and **visits to SOS villages and community-based homes are not permitted**.

### Village / area selection
The online form includes a field asking where the donor wishes to support children. Options seen: **Gammarth, Siliana, Mahres, Akouda, El Alâa (Kairouan), Hassi El Ferid (Kasserine)**.

- The public page does **not** explain whether this is strict earmarking or a preference for allocation.
- It is a **location choice, not a choice of an individual child**.
- **Do not promise** that money is restricted to a precise child, activity or location unless SOS confirms in writing.

## 5. The donor journey, step by step

1. **Introduction** – explain who SOS is, the child-protection mission and what a monthly PAP contribution does. Keep it accurate and brief.
2. **Donor questions** – give the donor time to ask about amount, withdrawal timing, flexibility, privacy and how to contact SOS.
3. **Amount and start month** – the donor chooses an amount they can comfortably afford and the first month of debit.
4. **Authorization details** – the donor completes the form, provides required personal or company information and bank details/RIB, and signs.
5. **Submission** – the completed authorization is sent via the official online form or the current approved paper/app process provided by SOS.
6. **Processing and first debit** – SOS processes the mandate. The public page says debits are scheduled for the 25th; the donor should **confirm when the first debit will actually occur**.
7. **Ongoing support** – the recurring payment provides more predictable resources. The donor can contact SOS to update the amount, pause or stop.
8. **Follow-up** – keep the donor informed using approved SOS communications. **Do not invent** reports, receipts or impact figures.

> **Consent must be real.** The donor must understand that the contribution is recurring, know the amount, understand the debit method and choose to authorize it. **Never complete a bank mandate for someone without clear, informed permission.**

## 6. Understanding the authorization form

The live form is titled **"Contrat d'autorisation de prélèvement automatique permanent"**. It includes fields for the contribution, donor details, bank coordinates, first debit month and signature. The interface may change – follow the live form and current staff instructions.

| Form area | What it asks for | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| Contribution amount | Amount in figures **and** amount written in words. | Both must match exactly. Confirm it is a **monthly** contribution, not a one-time gift. |
| Zakat intention | Yes/no: is the contribution intended as Zakat? | **Do not select it on the donor's behalf.** For religious-eligibility questions, refer to SOS's approved guidance or a qualified adviser. |
| Village/area preference | List of villages/areas. | Explain it is a location choice, not a named child. Don't promise strict earmarking unless SOS confirms. |
| Donor type | *Personne physique* or *Société*. | Select the correct category. Companies may be asked for legal name, tax ID and responsible person. |
| Personal/contact details | Name, profession, address, email, phone. Company: company name, tax ID, responsible contact. | Check spelling; make sure SOS can contact the donor about the mandate. |
| Bank information | Bank-related details and RIB. | Must be a **Tunisian current account**. Enter/provide details only through an approved secure process. |
| First debit month | The donor selects the first month. | Make sure the expected first collection month is understood; don't promise an exact processing date unless confirmed. |
| Signature | Signature of the sponsor/donor. | The donor must **personally** approve. An unsigned mandate may not be valid for processing. |

> **Data protection in practice.** A RIB and personal contact details are sensitive. **Never** photograph, copy, message or store a donor's banking details on a personal device or personal account. Use only SOS-approved systems and retention procedures.

## 7. How the monthly withdrawal works

### Published schedule
- **Normal monthly debit:** the **25th** of each month, from the donor's Tunisian current account.
- **Second attempt** (only if the first fails for insufficient balance): the **5th of the following month**.
- **Missed contribution:** **not carried forward**; failed debits are not cumulative.

| Event | Published timing | Meaning for the donor |
|---|---|---|
| Normal monthly debit | 25th of each month | The chosen monthly amount is scheduled for collection. |
| Second attempt if first fails (insufficient balance) | 5th of the following month | The association tries once more after the failed first attempt. |
| Missed contribution | Not carried forward | The missed amount is not added to the next month's debit. |

### Worked example
A donor authorizes **TND 25/month**. Debit is attempted on **25 February**. If it fails for insufficient funds, a second attempt is made on **5 March**. If that also fails, the failed February amount is **not** automatically added to the March debit. The normal monthly schedule continues; the donor should contact SOS if they need to update details or contribution.

### Accepted payment methods
The public sponsorship page says the PAP route is **exclusively** automatic debit from a **Tunisian current account**; **bank-card payments are not accepted** for this route. This does **not** mean SOS has no other donation channels (its website describes other ways to give). Keep PAP distinct from one-time online donations, SMS campaigns and bank transfers.

**Explain the schedule exactly:**
> "The published schedule is the 25th of each month, with a second attempt on the 5th of the following month if the first attempt fails for insufficient balance. The association says a missed debit is not carried forward."

## 8. Changing, pausing, stopping or updating details

- **Change the amount:** the donor can modify it at any time by contacting the team. They should state the new monthly amount and ask when it takes effect. **Do not promise** a change requested right before a debit will apply to that same debit.
- **Pause temporarily:** the donor may suspend the sponsorship temporarily. They should contact SOS and ask for confirmation of pause dates and the restart date (if a restart is intended).
- **Stop:** the donor can stop at any time by contacting the team. "Permanent" in PAP describes a recurring standing arrangement – **not** a commitment that cannot be cancelled.
- **Change bank/account/RIB:** provide the new bank details so the team can update the mandate and avoid interruption. Use an **approved secure channel**; do **not** ask the donor to send a RIB via informal personal messaging.

### Good practice after a request
- Record the date and nature of the donor's request in the official SOS system, following internal rules.
- Give the correct official contact route and explain the team will process the request.
- Encourage the donor to keep SOS's confirmation and check later bank statements.
- If a debit occurs after a stop/change request, direct the donor promptly to the responsible SOS team. Don't argue or assume processing status.

### Contact information
- The SOS sponsorship page displays the public phone number **+216 58 371 002**.
- Use the live **Contact** page or official channels to confirm the current **email** and processing contact before giving a donor an address.

## 9. How to explain PAP in a conversation

### Conversation structure

| Stage | Goal | Example wording |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Ask permission, introduce the cause. | "Hello. We are introducing the work of SOS Villages d'Enfants Tunisie and a way to support children through a regular monthly contribution. May I explain it briefly?" |
| Mission | Explain who is helped. | "SOS works with children who have lost parental care or are at risk of losing it, and also supports vulnerable families so children can grow in a safer environment." |
| Mechanism | Explain what PAP is. | "PAP means a monthly automatic debit from a Tunisian current account. You choose the amount and the first month." |
| Control | Explain flexibility and timing. | "The published schedule is the 25th of each month. You can contact SOS to change, pause or stop your contribution." |
| Limits | Handle limits honestly. | "It is support for SOS's work; it does not mean choosing a named child or receiving access to a child." |
| Close | Let the person decide freely. | "Would you like to look at the official form and decide whether an amount is comfortable for you?" |

### 45-second script
> "SOS Villages d'Enfants Tunisie supports children who need protection and works with vulnerable families to help children grow up in a safe and stable environment. PAP is a voluntary monthly contribution by automatic debit from a Tunisian current account. You choose the amount and the first month. The published debit date is the 25th of the month. The contribution helps SOS plan its work over time. It is not sponsorship of a named child, because children's identities are protected. And you can contact SOS to change, pause or stop the contribution. I can show you the official form so you can review everything before deciding."

### Making the explanation trustworthy
- Start with the child-protection mission, **not** the bank details.
- Explain the recurring nature **before** asking the donor to fill in any form.
- Say "monthly contribution" repeatedly; don't let the donor think it is a single payment.
- State the amount and withdrawal date clearly, and point to the official form.
- Let the donor read the form and ask questions. **Silence is not consent.**
- Accept a refusal politely.

## 10. Questions and objections: honest answers

**"I do not want a permanent commitment."**
> "That makes sense. PAP is recurring, but SOS says you can contact its team to change, pause or stop it at any time. You should only choose an amount and start date that you are comfortable with."

**"Can I choose the child I sponsor?"**
> "No. SOS protects children's personal information and does not disclose the identity of a specific child. The form does show a village/area option, but that is not the same as choosing a named child."

**"Can I visit the child or the village?"**
> "SOS's public page says visits to SOS villages and community-based homes are not permitted, to protect children's privacy."

**"Why not just give cash once?"**
> "A one-time gift is also valuable. PAP is different because it creates more predictable monthly support, which can help SOS plan its work over time. You can choose whichever giving method is right for you."

**"What if I have no money in my account that day?"**
> "The public page says a second attempt is made on the 5th of the following month if the first attempt on the 25th fails for insufficient balance. It also says missed debits are not carried forward."

**"Can I pay by card?"**
> "For the PAP sponsorship route, SOS's public page says payment is by automatic debit from a Tunisian current account, not by bank card. SOS may offer other donation routes separately."

**"Is there a minimum amount?"**
> "An SOS press release in October 2024 mentioned TND 10 as the starting amount. The current online form I checked does not display a minimum, so I would confirm the current threshold with the association rather than guess."

**"How do I stop it?"**
> "SOS says you can stop at any time by contacting its team. I can direct you to the official contact details so the request is handled by the association."

**"Where exactly will my money go?"**
> "It supports the association's work for children and families. I can explain the programme areas described by SOS, but I should not promise that a specific amount pays for one named item or one child unless SOS publishes that allocation."

**"Is it Zakat?"**
> "The current form asks whether the contribution is intended as Zakat. I should not give a religious ruling or select that option for you. Please review the association's approved guidance or ask a qualified religious adviser if this is important to you."

## 12. Ethics, privacy and child safeguarding

### Do
- Tell the truth about how PAP works, including its monthly nature and withdrawal date.
- Make sure the donor understands and **personally authorizes** the amount and bank debit.
- Use only current official SOS forms, approved communication materials and authorized channels.
- Respect a person's right to decline, take time or ask someone else before deciding.
- Use child-safe language that preserves each child's dignity – talk about rights, protection, education and family stability, **not** pity or sensationalism.
- Protect bank details and other personal information; follow SOS data-security, retention and access rules.

### Do not
- Promise a named child, private updates, personal contact or a visit to a village/home.
- Invent success stories, costs, statistics, funding percentages or specific outcomes.
- Say the donor is legally unable to stop (SOS says the contribution can be stopped at any time by contacting the team).
- Fill in a donor's amount, Zakat choice, bank details or signature without their clear instruction and consent.
- Collect RIBs on a personal phone, save photos of bank documents, or share donor information with unauthorized people.
- Use shame, guilt, frightening claims or false urgency to secure a PAP.

> **Ethical standard:** a successful PAP is not merely a completed form. It is a donor who understands the commitment, chooses freely, trusts the information and knows how to contact SOS later.
